# Props that act

**Character Designer** · v1 · drawings in `props.svg`. All four props are built to tier C (full detail, see `../style-guide.md`) because each gets an insert.

## 1. Noor's lanyard and badge

- **Strap:** 20 mm woven polyester, **THE RED `#C8102E`**, tonal "A&L" repeat woven in (same hue). Loop ~90 cm so she can pull it over her head in one move. **No breakaway clasp**: a popping clasp would make the throw read as an accident instead of a choice.
- **Clip:** steel swivel hook, clear badge holder.
- **Badge:** white PVC `#EEEEE8`, CR80 portrait. Slate logo bar `#4B5867` (no red on the badge itself). Photo: her "fine" smile, mouth only, a year younger.
- **Small print:** "ID 40217 · VALID TO 09/2026". It's a fair-play clue for a second viewing (she was let go in September). It is never featured or pushed in on.
- **Back:** plain white, dark magnetic stripe, "If found, please return to reception." This is what we see when she turns it over in her fingers during Walter's speech.
- **Placeholders for the Director to confirm:** surname "Aziz" (deliberately not starting with S, so it can't collide with "My name is S—") and the company "Ashgrove & Lyle".
- **Acting beats:** straightened face-out on boarding (cue 0 tick), turned over during Walter's speech, yanked off and thrown on the floor in the creeping dark. **On the floor it must stay readable as red** in the dark section.

## 2. Walter's newspaper and pen

- **Paper:** broadsheet folded to a quarter, newsprint `#D9D3C3`, ink `#2B2B2B`. Fictional masthead "THE EVENING LEDGER" in **black**. No colour photos on any visible page. Printed text is greeked so it can't compete with the handwriting.
- **The fold** is soft and worn: the same inside page every night.
- **Margin notes** (one prop per beat): Walter's "DON'T LIE IN HERE." is in tall, careful capitals. Bea's "IT'S AWAKE NOW. YOU WOKE IT." goes underneath in a rounder, quicker hand. Both are blue-black ballpoint `#1F2A44`. Letters are ~25 mm tall so they read at 40–50 mm across the car.
- **Pen:** black barrel, black cap, steel tip. No coloured cap.
- **Acting beats:** folded and not read; lowered completely (lie 2); set on the knee to write; the crease folded before the speech; **unfolded and the page turned** at the end (first time).

## 3. The phones

| | Noor | Kai |
|---|---|---|
| Case | Navy `#2A3140` | Clear grey `#8E9399`, cracked screen protector (bottom right) |
| Incoming | **MARK**, light blue-white screen, grey decline / pale accept. **No red** | **MUM**, slide to answer. **No red** |
| In call | "Mark", timer, **dark grey end button** `#5A6170` | "Mum", timer, **THE RED end-call button** `#C8102E`: the film's second and last red |
| Light | Lights her face from below in the final dark stretch; the phone side becomes the key when she answers | Its small blue glow stays alive in the dark section (cue 7) |
| End state | Pressed to her ear on the platform | Dims to ~30%, then goes dark in the final shot |

- Screens are emissive, low brightness, cold blue-white (`#D6E4FF` glow). The UI is generic: no real OS, no logos.
- **Kai's call timer keeps running all film.** It's a clock: when Noor hovers over the button around the 8-minute mark it reads about 3:00.
- **The 40 mm insert:** Noor's thumb over Kai's red button, lit only by the screen and the overhead panel. The red must stay readable. It rhymes with her strap.
