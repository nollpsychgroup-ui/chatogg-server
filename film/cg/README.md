# film/cg: carriage set, painterly look-dev, light rig, previs

**Owner:** 3D / Technical Artist · Milestone 3 · Blender 5.3 (headless; also runs in the `bpy` 5.3 wheel)

The Python scripts are the source of truth, and renders are low-res previews. No `.blend` is committed, because `build_scene.py` rebuilds the whole film-length scene in about a second.

## Set (DP's axes)

Z is up. **Y runs down the car from end wall B (y=0) to end wall A (y=13, Noor)**; side A is −X and side B is +X. The car is 13 × 2.8 × 2.3 m, plus a vestibule at y −4…0. P1 sits at y=12.458 above Noor, with a 1.083 m pitch to P12 at y=0.542. Sliding doors are on side B between P6 and P7. Walter (side A) and Bea (side B) sit under P3, Kai sits under P8 by the doors, and Noor has the transverse seat against wall A, facing down the car toward Pos A.

## Look-dev: painted world, real light

1. **Only real emitters light the scene.** These are P1–P12, the display, the phone screens, the door indicator and the platform lamp. The world is black (0), with no fill and no light painted into a texture.
2. **Paint lives on the surfaces.** `PaintSurface` breaks albedo into flat, quantised brush dabs, so the **paint is posterised and the light never is.** Strokes are locked to object space and run along a set axis per material: down the car on side walls, floor and benches, vertical on the end walls. Stroke size follows the Designer's tiers: set ~5 cm, costume ~2 cm, faces ~5 mm. Surfaces are matte gouache (roughness 0.8–0.9). Gloss is limited to the corneas, badge plastic, phone glass and steel trim.
3. **The terminator breaks up** through a small stroke-shaped normal perturbation fixed to the surface, so it responds to the real light and never moves. Characters get no relief.
4. **Compositor:** an anisotropic Kuwahara pass (size 7 on the set, 3 on faces and hands via an Object Index mask, so lids, brows and mouth corners stay crisp). A canvas tooth goes on **the set only, as a multiply**, so black × anything stays 0. There's no bloom, vignette, glow or Freestyle.
5. **No boil.** Cycles uses a fixed seed (no animated seed) and strokes never move, so two asleep frames are identical (check L1/8d). The Director's optional awake-only boil test is not built. It's parked, because "no boil" is the approved baseline.
6. **Colour:** looks come from `cues.json` as level plus Kelvin. The camera is white-balanced to 4000 K, so BASE 4300 K with green reads as cool transit light, BROWN 2200 K as sodium, DIP 3200 K as a warm near-black, and the ~5000 K platform lamp as cold white. The only reds are the strap and Kai's end-call button (`THE_RED`). The display is the Designer's cold white-green, `#CFE8D8`.

## Look-dev v2 (Director R1/R2 + the human's "stronger paint" note)

- **Brush dabs in every surface** (`PaintSurface` v2): two overlapping Voronoi layers of elongated, wobbly dabs, locked to object space. Each dab has one value quantised to 4 painted steps, a hue shift, bristle streaks along the stroke, and **its own normal tilt**. The tilt makes the real terminator break into brush shapes, and in flat light it gives colour variation within a plane. The light itself is never quantised, so the ±3% breathing and the dips stay smooth.
- **Painted board in the comp:** a static field of tapered, oriented brush strokes (about 2.5% of frame width long) **multiplied** over the set. It's set-only, so brushwork reads at every distance, including the Meter. It's static, so asleep frames stay bit-identical, and it's a multiply, so black stays 0. Kuwahara is reduced to 4 on the set and 2 on faces, so it no longer smooths the strokes away.
- **Faceted figures:** the blocking mannequins are flat-shaded planes in costume strokes.
- **Heads: the Character Designer's face rig** (`design/rig/heads.py`, all four characters, every face-rig.md control on `<NAME>_HEADRIG`). It's built with the TA's painted skin, hair and lip materials, and placed on the DP's marks. Bea's eyes-closed state is set through the rig (`lid_up` 0). A luminance-masked larger Kuwahara merges the darks into quieter shapes, and the rails are satin steel (DP).
- **Noor's torso** comes from `lib/noor.py`, which also holds the earlier TA head sculpt; that sculpt is now superseded by the rig (`lib/noor.py`, to `design/sheets/noor.svg`): an egg cranium, narrowed jaw, shallow sockets and brow for top light, a nose wedge, lips and a firm mouth line, and ears. Real eyes: pale sclera, mid-dark iris, and a glossy refracting cornea that takes the panel catchlights. The upper lid sits at her neutral `lid_up` 0.82, with a firm lid line and brows. Her hair goes back to a low bun, with an optional loose lock. The blazer has squared padded shoulders and lapels open over the blouse, with the strap and badge dead centre. The face-rig controls (`lid_up`, `brow_in`, `smile`, `press`, `gaze`) are build parameters for now and become shape keys in M4.
- **R2:** wall A behind Noor is a darker end-wall panel (0.09 × the laminate value; it sits 0.5 m from P1), with +0.25 EV and eyes on the upper third for sf06. Measured display luminance is 0.53 for the face against 0.38 for the wall, so the wall sits about a stop under, done without fill.

## Light rig

- **P1–P12** are area lights named exactly as the cue numbering, 0.64 × 0.6 m at z=2.29, with spread 100° and a short skirt. Each carries `["level"]`, `["rgb"]`, `["breath"]` and `["partial"]`. Drivers feed both the light and its visible diffuser, so they can never disagree.
- **Breathing:** `W × level × (1 + depth × awake × breath × sin(2π·frame / period))`, with depth and period read from `cues.json` (shared with the hum). It's on from cue 1 to 8.10, and P1 steadies (stops breathing) at 8.0.
- **Hard edge (L5): virtual baffles.** The set is split at the panel joins. Each panel's *direct* light is linked to its own section, plus all characters and props. The floor, seats and walls therefore get a hard edge at the join, while faces at the edge still catch the neighbour's spill, so Walter and Bea are rim-lit from P2 after 7.10, as the lighting plan asks. Bounce is not restricted.
- **Keys** land on whole frames (frame = round(t × 24), frame 0 = 0 s) and are CONSTANT, except the DP's named eases: the cue 3 sag, the cue 5 recovery and the cue 8 overshoot settle.
- **Practical channels:** `CTRL_DISPLAY`, `CTRL_WINDOWS` (passing lights, visible only in glass: camera, glossy and transmission rays, never diffuse, and transparent outside the bands), `CTRL_PLATFORM` (the lamp is linked to the platform and characters only), `CTRL_DOOR`, `CTRL_PHONE_NOOR` and `CTRL_PHONE_KAI`. The doors slide and the platform rig slides in and out of the door glass from the events.
