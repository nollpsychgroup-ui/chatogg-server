# THE QUIET CAR: Shot List

**DP** · Milestone 3 · Built on the locked `script/draft-02.md` · Read with `camera/lighting-plan.md` (set plan, panels P1–P12, cue numbers) and `camera/storyboards/index.html` (frames for the key beats).

## How the film is covered

1. **The Meter (Pos A) runs the whole film.** Before any coverage, we shoot the full script in **real time, end to end, from Pos A**, with the complete cue stack and sound partners firing. We aim for 3–4 full runs. That gives us the spine of the edit (every escalation cuts back to this frame, per the meter rule), a safety cut of the whole film, and a full rehearsal for the board operator, the actors and the Composer's stems. In the list, shots marked **A-RUN** come from these runs.
2. **Every cue is visible in the frame it plays in.** Each shot that contains a cue has the cued panel(s) in frame, or shows the cue's light landing on a face. We never cut *to* a panel in reaction to a cue. We cut to the Meter *after* the cue, to count the damage.
3. **The ceiling enters at the first lie.** Singles before cue 1 frame the ceiling out. From shot 6 onward, every frame that can hold the ceiling does, until 8.10.
4. **Lenses:** 25 · 32 · 40 · 50 · **75 (only shots 58 and 63, the confession)**.
5. **Movement:** locked off, except for **shot 19** (the only handheld), **shot 44** (the creep push, Pos A dolly) and **shot 58** (the confession push, which lands before 8.10). All three happen while the car is awake.

## Setups and schedule (proposal for the Producer)

The lighting state is driven entirely by the cue stack, so **any position can run any part of the film** in story order. We group setups by position and by how much reset each needs, not by lighting.

| Day | Setups | Shots |
|---|---|---|
| **0, test day** | camera and lighting tests T1–T10 (`lighting-plan.md` §6); blocking; the Composer's stems on QLab | — |
| **1, the Meter** | Pos A: the full real-time A-RUN ×3–4 · the dedicated vanishing takes (16) ×4–6 · the creep dolly (44) ×3 · the bookend (68) | 1, 9, 16, 28, 31, 44, 53, 59, 68 |
| **2, the people** | Pos C and D (the Noor, Walter and Bea singles) · E two-shots · F (Kai) · G handheld (19) | 2–3, 5–8, 10–11, 13–15, 17, 19–20, 23, 25–27, 29, 34–42, 46–52, 55, 57, 61, 65–66 |

**Three shooting days plus a test day.** Kai (the actor) is only needed on day 1 and the first half of day 2. The platform flat and the wagon are needed only on day 3.
