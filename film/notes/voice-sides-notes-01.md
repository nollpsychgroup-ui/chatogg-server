# Director's Notes: script/voice-sides.md

**Verdict: APPROVED.** These sides are precise, faithful to the locked script, and exactly what an animation-first record needs. The typed cut-offs are especially good, because each cut-off becomes a different frame timing for the animators. So is the "long version past the cut" rule. The story-order ensemble with the cue stack in cans matches the treatment's recording plan.

## The three ⚑ items

**1. Seven voices: confirmed.** My recommendation to the Producer is a **separate actor for the Automated Voice**. It's one line, so it's cheap. Don't double it with Mark or Mum. Even heavily processed, a shared timbre risks the audience subconsciously linking the car to someone who loves Noor or Kai. The car must belong to no one. If the budget forces a double, the least harmful option is a crew or session voice with no other role.

**2. Phone-bed text and non-verbals: approved as non-script performance scaffolding**, on these conditions:
- **They must never be intelligible in the mix** (except Mum's "Kai?", as scripted). I'll check this in the mix.
- **MARK-04:** the scaffold text "Come home" leans toward forgiveness. Since the ending withholds his reply, give the actor **a neutral scaffold as well** (for example: "Noor. ... Okay. ... I'm— okay. Where are you now?"), and record warm, neutral and strained versions on both texts. I'll choose the most ambiguous in the mix.
- **MARK-03 (the 20 s of listening on an open line):** this is excellent. Keep it.
- **Non-verbals NOOR-00 to NOOR-18 and WALTER-00, and wild tracks NV-01 to NV-07:** approved. NV-06 (Walter's own page turn) should be the version used in the final mix if it's clean. It's the last human sound in the film.

**3. Automated Voice alts ("Now arriving..." and "Next stop..."): not approved for use.** The PA must speak **only once** in the film. Its single line lands because the car has been silent in words until then. Record the alts as safety if it costs nothing, but they don't go in the cut. The display carries station information.
