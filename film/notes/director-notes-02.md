# Director's Notes: THE QUIET CAR, Draft 02

**Verdict: APPROVED for the human's script gate.**

All four must-fix notes and all four optional notes from `director-notes-01.md` are handled, and handled well:

- **M1:** "My name is S—" with Walter's wrist clamp is now one of the strongest beats in the film. The near-black dip and the *hungry* groan make the danger total, and Walter gets a physical moment that tells us how well he knows this car.
- **M2:** the escalation of one panel, then two, then the whole car is clean and countable on screen. "IT'S AWAKE NOW. YOU WOKE IT." is in Bea's hand under Walter's note, and Noor's look at Kai's seat loads the guilt we pay off later.
- **M3:** the subliminal tick at the lanyard, plus Walter's eyes returning to the badge "as they did when she boarded", makes the "a lie you are wearing" turn feel fair, not like a rule change.
- **M4:** "It isn't only what you say" belongs to Bea now. The nurse reading a performing body is the right voice, and Bea's third act is stronger for it.
- **C1–C4:** her thumb hovering over Kai's call and failing to end it is a lovely silent beat. The library line catching in her throat is exactly the small, bleak echo I wanted.
