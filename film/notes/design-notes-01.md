# Director's Notes: design/ (style guide, sheets, expressions, props)

**Verdict: APPROVED for Milestone 3**, with two follow-ups (below) that can be done during M4 previs.

The style guide is the best articulation yet of "the world is painted; the light is real." The key decisions are all exactly right:
- no light baked into textures;
- **glossy corneas that reflect the real panel row**, so the catchlights go out panel by panel in cue 7. This is a superb idea, and it makes Noor's eyes a second panel meter;
- faces designed for top light, with no fill;
- lit edges firm, shadow edges lost, features last to go;
- multiply-only overlays, so the blackout is 0,0,0;
- one red, `#C8102E`, on the strap and Kai's end-call button only, with Noor's own end-call button grey;
- an acting range of 5–25%, with the peaks capped.

The Hopper and Hammershøi touchstones are the right ones for the whole crew.

## Brush boil

My treatment asked for a *test* of a faint boil only while the car is awake. Your guide rules **no boil, ever** ("a shimmering brush texture would read as a light event"). **I accept that as the baseline.** It's the cleaner rule. The TA may still show me the awake-only test in look-dev, but unless it's unmistakably "the room breathing," surface-locked strokes stand.

## Your five rulings

1. **Badge "NOOR AZIZ / Ashgrove & Lyle": approved.** The surname not starting with S is good thinking, since it doesn't muddy "My name is S—".
2. **"VALID TO 09/2026" small print: approved**, as a never-featured second-viewing clue. It's literally a lie she wears. Keep it below legibility in every planned shot, including the 40 mm insert.
3. **No hospital ID for Bea: approved.** Only one badge in the car.
4. **No breakaway clasp: approved.** The throw is a choice, and it's built long enough (90 cm loop) to come off over her head in one pull.
5. **Skin tones and looks are provisional pending voice casting: approved.** Casting is voice-first and open. Designs shouldn't be locked to an actor's appearance, but I'll review once the voices are cast.

## Follow-ups (during M4, not blocking)

**F1. Seated silhouettes.** The lineup's silhouette test is all *standing*, and standing, Kai doesn't read as the slouched triangle and Walter doesn't lean. Three of our four characters spend the whole film **seated**, and the DP's wides are down the length of the car. Please add a **seated silhouette row** in each character's signature posture: Kai slouched with knees apart and head down to his phone, Walter stooped behind the paper, Bea upright with her hands on the bag and eyes shut, Noor upright and presenting. Those are the silhouettes the audience will actually read.

**F2. Bea's face in the dark states.** In the greyscale check, Bea's face sits close in value to her navy scrubs. Under the brownout (cue 3) and at the edge of the creeping dark, her face must still read. Her eyes-closed-but-listening acting depends on it. Keep the oatmeal cardigan framing her face, and ask the TA to include Bea in the brownout style frame as an explicit check. If needed, lift her *sclera and lid-line contrast* (not her skin value) so the acting zones survive.
